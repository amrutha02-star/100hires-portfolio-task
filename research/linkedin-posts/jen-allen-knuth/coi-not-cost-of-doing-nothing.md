# Cost of inaction is not the cost of doing nothing

Author: Jen Allen-Knuth
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/demandjen1_ffs-cost-of-inaction-coi-is-not-the-cost-activity-7435701620342005760-z3bO
Published: approx. March 2026 (about three months old when checked)
Date checked: June 2026

---

FFS, cost of inaction (COI) is not the "cost of doing nothing".

If it's a significant business problem, the business is doing SOMETHING about it.

So, when we say things like, "What's at risk if you continue to do nothing for the next 6 months?"...

...it's a bit like a personal trainer saying, "What's at risk if you continue to do nothing?" to someone who has been running 3 miles/day every morning at 5 am, but not losing weight.

It's insulting and it lacks empathy.

They're not doing "nothing". How they're currently solving the problem might just be built off flawed or outdated beliefs/assumptions.

When done well, COI is an empathetic motion.

We're not pointing our finger at the prospect. It's not a 'gotcha' moment.

We're looking at the business problem together. We're labeling what changed in the environment that caused that initial assumption to no longer be true.
