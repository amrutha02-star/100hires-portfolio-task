# Most cold calls should not turn into conversations

Author: Kevin Hopp
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/khopp_coldcalling-outbound-coldcall-activity-7468728252216487936-LOIW
Published: June 5, 2026 (a few days old when checked)
Date checked: 8 and 9 June 2026 (IST)

---

My hottest take about cold calling is...

That most cold calls should not turn into conversations.

Yes, that is the GOAL of calling in the first place.

But MOST of the people you call are doing something else in that moment.

Forcing a conversation leads to so many of what I call "False Negatives"

A false negative is when your prospect gets pitch-slammed and they just use a gut reflex response "Hey, let me stop you there, we're all set. We're all good. We are actually not interested, THANK YOU for the call! **Hangs up**"

This is why I teach cold callers to be so so sensitive of other people's time.

Permission-based openers aren't an option - they are a necessity.

Probably 55% of the calls that we get beeped into via Orum at SmartBound result in us just marking the lead as "no answer" and us calling the lead back another day.

The goal isn't a conversation RIGHT NOW when you want it to happen - the goal is a good two-dialogue when the person you want to call is ready for the call, and has 2-3 minutes to actually talk.
