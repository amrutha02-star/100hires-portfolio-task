To start, I used ZoomInfo GTM Studio to build two super niche lists.

Save this post so you can follow the step-by-step guide for your next outbound campaign.


First, my logic -

In outbound, more is not a strategy; it is SPAM.

This is what I teach my clients so they can run micro-campaigns. The goal is to break their account list into manageable segments.

By segmenting an account list, it's possible to write relevant messaging without having to fall back to contact-level personalization (which is most often boring and bad BS like saw you follow [competitor] on LinkedIn  OR see you are [job title] at ABC company).

In nearly 20 years of running sales teams & building outbound strategies, this is the only way I've found to successfully personalize at scale.

Even though I am not scaling, the same logic applies to my outreach, & GTM Studio makes it super easy to create dialed-in account lists.



👉 My process -

1. Identify my audience

Before outbounding for this refreshed version of my Done-With-You Advisory product, I reviewed my most impactful work over the past 4 years.

I wanted to figure out not just who *could* buy from me, but who was best positioned to buy (money, need & timing), get massive ROI, & become a testimonial/referral source in the future.

What I uncovered:

- B2B SaaS & Professional Services
- Revenue between 50M - 500M
- Based in the US or Canada
- Revenue strategy = outbound as a critical source of net new revenue
- Signal = undergoing a significant market shift

Before I logged onto #ZoomInfo to build an audience, I went even deeper.

I know that two of my strongest case studies are with Canadian SaaS orgs that were going through a period of rapid acquisition (M&A).



2. Create Audience

- Company using ZoomInfo data
- Applied filters (industry: software, revenue: 50M-500M, location: Canada)
- Added a scoop ✨ this is where the magic happens✨ Navigate to "financial scoops" and select Mergers & Acquisitions

Using the filters, I was at 588 Accounts.

That is too many for me, but it could be a great place for a larger company to start with a social proof cadence.

With the added M&A scoop, I reduced the list to 20 Accounts.


3. Enrich my Audience List

I used the #GTMStudio chat feature to get the list ready for outbound.

Here's what I added:

- A column of the specific M&A news so that I could easily validate if the account was actually a good fit based on my super niche criteria

- A tab with all of the CROs & VPs of Sales for these orgs.


4. Get Eyes on the List

I did a quick manual review / clean up to ensure I had the right titles from the right types of accounts.
