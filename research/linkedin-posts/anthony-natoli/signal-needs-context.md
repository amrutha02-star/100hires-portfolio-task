# A signal is not a reason to reach out, the problem behind it is

Author: Anthony Natoli
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/anthony-natoli_outbound-is-not-dead-most-people-are-just-activity-7458177020452192256-avci
Published: May 7, 2026
Date checked: June 2026

---

Outbound is not dead... Most people are just terrible at it.

Because somewhere along the way, we lost the plot with outbound.

Everyone is now obsessed with signal-based prospecting. But almost no one knows how to actually do it.

Just like when sales leaders gave their teams Sequencer Tools and they all ended up building long, dusty automated sequences that just annoyed prospects.

So with signal lead outbound, prospects are now inboxes are flooded with AI slop dressed up as personalization. Cold calls are worse because reps think a hiring signal counts as research.

Prospects have built mental spam filters and they spot the laziness in two seconds.

Having the 'signal' or 'intent' is not enough.

A funding round is not a reason to reach out. A new hire is not a reason to reach out. A product launch is not a reason to reach out.

Signal based prospecting DOES work when done correctly.. But it requires a human and some manual effort.

The reason to reach out is the problem & context behind the signal.

→ They raised a Series B. Okay, what's the 'why' behind the raise? How will they deploy the capital? What problems come when an org usually raises a round at their stage?
→ They're hiring a VP of RevOps. Okay, what does that role actually own, and why did they need one now? What OKRs are they responsible for?
→ They're moving upmarket. Okay, what does this actually mean? What problems could they run into?
→ Someone posted on LinkedIn. NOT A SIGNAL!!!

What actually works..

Connecting the dots between the signal/observation and the problem you actually solve.

You find the common challenges & problems from customers you already work with..

And then go find prospects who also might have those same problems.

The reps booking meetings right now are the ones putting the human touch on top of the research.

When a prospect sees their message.. The signal has context relevant to THEM in the form of a problem.. Not some blanketed statement.

Am I crazy here?
