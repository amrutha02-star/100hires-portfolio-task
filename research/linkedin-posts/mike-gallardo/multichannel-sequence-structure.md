How to structure an outbound sequence:

(steal these 6 steps today)

1. Value Props

You need two. Spend a lot of time researching and thinking through the core value propositions of your startup. You should know, in your own customer's words, exactly what their challenges are, what they're looking to accomplish and how you specifically help.

2. Triple Tap

Email, phone and social all on the same day to break the noise. Best strategy for getting attention and responses from busy prospects. Plus, you never know which channel your prospect is most likely to engage with.

3. Cold Emails

Two emails per value proposition. The first email you send plus a reply email. That's it. Anything more and you start risking getting marked as SPAM. Focus on quality over quantity.

5. Cold Calls

6 total. No matter what anyone says these work. Tied for first with LinkedIn outreach right now. And If you have a power dialer it can be a superpower. Do not sleep on the phones, friends.

6. Break up email

This is optional but I think it can be done well. A lot of people have strong beliefs here and whenever that's the case my advice is to test it out yourself.

Bonus tip: Engage with their content on LinkedIn.
