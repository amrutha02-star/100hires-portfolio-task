# Outbound best practices from B2B SaaS companies

Author: Mark Colgan
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/outboundsalestech_outboundsales-b2bsales-salesstrategy-activity-7467175858944974848-TQ6P
Published: approx. June 2026 (about a week old when checked)
Date checked: 8 and 9 June 2026 (IST)

---

I've been collecting outbound best practices from leading B2B SaaS companies for the last few years.

Different stages, different ACVs, different team sizes. From 7 BDRs to 300+ SDRs.

Here's the TLDR of what the best teams are doing right now:

→ Account selection is the single biggest performance lever. Not messaging, not channels, not tools.

→ Email is roughly 15x harder than it was 18 months ago. Phone has stayed constant.

→ The highest-performing teams are getting 50-70% of meetings from cold calls. LinkedIn voice notes are getting 10-15% reply rates. Email alone is no longer enough.

→ Personalisation as a differentiator is dying. Every SDR now has the same AI research tools. What's replacing it: situational relevance (something happening in the buyer's world right now) and social proof (what their peers are doing differently).

→ AI works best in the gap between having an account on a list and picking up the phone. Research, enrichment, scoring, draft messaging. It does not work for fully automated cold outbound yet.

→ The consistent rule: make it work with people first, then automate.

→ Outbound culture matters more than outbound tactics. Teams that fix scripts without fixing leadership behaviour, expectations, and operating rhythm regress within a quarter.

→ The companies seeing the best results are investing in data quality early (connect rates jumping from 3% to 16%), warming accounts with marketing before SDRs reach out (3.6x improvement in meeting rates), and measuring incrementality rather than attribution.

This is pulled from ongoing research across company case studies, GTM practitioner interviews, industry summits, and newsletter analysis.

I combine this with my own observations from working with 250+ B2B sales teams.
