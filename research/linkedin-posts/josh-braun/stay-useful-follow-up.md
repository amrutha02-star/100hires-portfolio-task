# Stay useful instead of checking in

Author: Josh Braun
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/josh-braun_just-checking-in-following-up-bumping-activity-7469483478900736000-BYsm
Published: approx. June 2026 (one day old when checked)
Date checked: June 2026

---

Just checking in.

Following up.

Bumping this up.

Wanted to circle back.

If the timing is off, reminding someone you exist doesn't change the timing.

It just reminds them you're desperate.

A better approach:

Stay useful.

Instead of asking for an update, send something about that makes them smarter about a topic they're interested in.

"This report on churn signals caught my eye. Sharing since you mentioned retention was a focus this year."

When timing is off, your job isn't to push the deal forward.

It's to stay top of mind.

Because timing changes.
Budgets get approved.
Priorities shift.
Problems get worse.

And when they do, the person who stayed useful is usually remembered before the person who kept checking in.
