# Neutral questions open doors

Author: Josh Braun
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/josh-braun_people-want-to-feel-capable-so-when-a-message-activity-7468615241896083457-_NN8
Published: approx. June 2026 (a few days old when checked)
Date checked: 8 and 9 June 2026 (IST)

---

People want to feel capable.

So when a message tells them they're not, something in them closes.

Compare these two approaches:

This closes a door:

"I was looking at your site and noticed you're not capturing email addresses. You're losing leads every day. That's what we help with."

It sounds helpful.

But underneath is:

"I see what you don't."
"I know what you don't."
"You're doing it wrong."

Even if it's true.

Now this opens a door:

"I didn't see an email signup on your site. How are you staying in touch with visitors who aren't ready to buy?"

Same observation.

Different energy.

The first delivers a conclusion.

The second invites a conversation.

A neutral question points toward a problem without pinning it on the person.

Explaining builds a ladder, with you at the top..

The moment someone feels judged, they close.

The moment they feel understood, they open.
