# Sources

Topic: Cold outreach pipeline for B2B SaaS

## Research question

How do B2B SaaS teams build cold outreach pipelines that create qualified replies and sales opportunities without relying on spammy volume?

## What this file includes

This file lists the experts selected for the research project and explains why they were chosen. The goal is to build a useful set of sources before turning the research into a real playbook.

For each expert, I am looking at:
- whether they have real experience in outbound, SaaS sales, growth, or founder-led sales
- whether their methods seem practical and believable
- whether they share examples, breakdowns, case studies, or lessons from practice
- whether their content is recent enough to be useful
- whether their material could help build a real playbook later

## How I selected experts

I did not want to pick 10 popular cold email names just because they are well known. Cold outreach is a full pipeline, so I looked for people who could help me understand different parts of it.

That includes:
- choosing the right target customers
- building a better lead list
- researching a prospect before reaching out
- writing a cold email or LinkedIn message that feels relevant
- following up without being annoying
- avoiding deliverability problems
- measuring replies, meetings booked, pipeline, or revenue
- using AI for research or drafting without making outreach generic

I also looked for trust signals. I wanted sources where there was some reason to believe the person’s methods had worked in practice, not just sounded good online. Follower count is a weak signal, and not enough by itself.

## Criteria I used

- Practitioner proof
- Tactical depth
- B2B SaaS relevance
- Recency
- Playbook usefulness
